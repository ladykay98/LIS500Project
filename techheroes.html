Mike's Tech Hero: Maria Ressa

As a former journalist and someone who’s interested in disinformation, when I reflect on the concept of a tech hero, I don’t necessarily think of s omeone who knows how to code let alone create a product. I think about those who hold Big Tech accountable and making sure that modern technology is safe for everyone to use.

Through that lens, one of my tech heroes is Maria Ressa, a Filipina journalist and Nobel Peace Prize laureate. She is the CEO of Rappler, a Filipino digital news outlet. In 2016, she investigated how then Philippine President Rodrigo Duterte used social media to spread disinformation by using paid followers and dummy accounts. Ressa ultimately became a target for the Duterte administration and looked for ways to punish her. She was eventually arrested for “cyber libel.” Her arrest was criticized by various human rights organizations.

I’ve seen interviews with Maria Ressa on various shows, including The Daily Show with Jon Stewart. But I was impacted by her book, How to Stand Up to a Dictator, which talks about her experience battling the Duterte administration and saw parallels on what is going on in America with the Trump administrations.

In her book, she tried to hold Mark Zuckerberg accountable for Facebook as a tool for spreading disinformation, which led to the election of Duterte. Those disinformation campaigns in the Philippines were replicated on a grander scale in the UK with Brexit and the first election of Donald Trump.
